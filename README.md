                        Post-Analysis insights /Recommendation
1. Regional concentration of top performers
The strongest results came from the North and South regions, led by Nancy Freehafer and Arne Larsen. This suggests stronger market demand or better customer penetration in these regions compared to the West, where the lowest performer is located.
2. Clear performance gap among salespersons
There is a significant gap between the top performer (Nancy: $104k) and the lowest performer (Jan: $16k). This indicates potential issues with territory potential, product mix, or individual sales effectiveness that require review and support.
3. Beverages dominate across most territories
Beverages consistently appear as the top-selling category for multiple salespersons (Arne, Jan, Michael, Nancy). This confirms beverages as the core revenue driver and a key category for future promotions and inventory focus.
4. Strong year-end sales momentum
December is the peak month for several salespersons (Arne, Mariya, Nancy, Andrew, Laura). This confirms seasonal demand spikes, likely linked to holidays, and suggests an opportunity for structured year-end campaigns.
5. June as a secondary peak period
June shows strong performance for multiple reps (Andrew, Jan, Laura, Robert), indicating a mid-year demand cycle that could be further leveraged through promotions.
6. Customer concentration risk
Most salespersons rely heavily on one or two major customers. For example, Andrew relies on Company D, and Nancy on Companies H and A. This creates a dependency risk if any major client reduces orders.
7. Product specialization by salesperson
Each salesperson shows a dominant product category (e.g., condiments for Laura, sauces for Mariya, dairy for Robert). This suggests opportunities to train salespersons to cross-sell higher-margin categories.
8. Underperforming territory requires intervention
The West region contains both mid- and low-performing salespersons, including the overall lowest performer. This suggests the need for territory review, customer expansion, or targeted support.

CONCLUSION

The analysis I carried out shows that sales are strongest in the North and South regions, with beverages as the main revenue driver and peak performance occurring in June and December. However, there are clear gaps in salesperson performance, underperformance in the West, and risks from heavy reliance on a few key customers. By focusing on high-performing areas, supporting weaker regions, expanding customer bases, and promoting cross-selling, the company can improve efficiency and achieve sustainable sales growth
